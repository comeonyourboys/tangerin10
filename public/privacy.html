Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Overview

Tangerine 10 ("we", "our", "the game") is a free browser-based number puzzle game available at tangerin10.web.app. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

Game Data: Your game scores and ranking name are stored in Firebase Realtime Database. No email, phone number, or personal identity information is collected.

Local Storage: We use your browser's local storage to save session history. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Third-Party Services

Google AdSense: We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.

Firebase: We use Google Firebase for hosting and database services. Firebase may collect usage data as described in Firebase Privacy Policy.

Kakao SDK: We use Kakao Share SDK for social sharing. Kakao's privacy policy applies when you use the share feature.

Cookies

We do not directly set cookies. Third-party services (Google AdSense, Firebase) may set cookies as described in their respective privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

This game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our GitHub repository.
