Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Overview

This policy explains what Tangerine 10 (tangerin10.web.app, "the site") stores, which third-party services it uses, and how advertising cookies work on it. The site is run by one individual; see the about page.

Who the site is for

Tangerine 10 is a general-audience website for people who enjoy number puzzles. It is not designed for or directed at children, and it does not ask for anyone's age.

Information we collect

Leaderboard entries. Only if your score reaches the top three and you press Save & Play Again, the name you typed (up to 12 characters), your score and the date are stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database and shown publicly on the leaderboard. Use a nickname if you do not want your real name shown.

Nothing else is collected by the game. There is no account, and the game never asks for an email address or phone number. The My History panel exists only in the open page and disappears when you reload it; the game does not write cookies or local storage on your device.

Advertising and cookies

Google AdSense. This site uses, or may use, Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings at https://adssettings.google.com. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting https://www.aboutads.info/choices. How Google uses information from sites that use its services is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Kakao AdFit. Some pages show ads from Kakao AdFit. Kakao may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. You can opt out of Kakao's personalised advertising on Kakao's targeted advertising page (in Korean); ads will still appear but may be less relevant.

The site itself does not set cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the game works without them.

Other third-party services

Firebase (Google). The site is hosted on Firebase Hosting and the leaderboard uses Firebase Realtime Database. Like any web server, Firebase receives your IP address and browser information when you load a page; see the Firebase privacy information.

Web fonts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net), which receive your IP address when they deliver them.

Kakao Share SDK. The game page loads Kakao's share library. When you use the Kakao share button, Kakao's privacy policy applies.

Children

Because the site is intended for a general audience, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (under 14 in Korea). If you believe a child has saved a leaderboard name that identifies them, email us and it will be removed.

Removing your data

To remove a leaderboard entry, email the name, score and date shown on the leaderboard. Entries are deleted, not edited.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: comeonyourboyz@gmail.com, or see the contact page.
