How to read these numbers

Only games where the player typed a name and saved are counted. A game finished without saving does not appear here, so these are counts of saved scores, not of everyone who played.

Scores are shown as entered. If a score looks impossible or a name is offensive, report it on the contact page and it will be removed.

Want to climb the board?

The fastest way up is keeping the combo alive. Quick small pairs inside the two-second window beat big rectangles by a wide margin. The strategy guide explains why.
