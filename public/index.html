About Tangerine 10

Tangerine 10 is a free browser puzzle built on one rule: drag a box around tangerines whose numbers add up to exactly ten, and they pop. You get 120 seconds. No sign-up, no download, no app store.

Every game deals a fresh grid of tangerines numbered 1 to 9 — 170 of them on a computer or tablet, 168 on a phone — so you never replay the same board. The My History panel shows your last five games for as long as this page stays open. If a score makes the top three, a name box appears, and the score goes on the leaderboard when you press Save & Play Again.

The rules in thirty seconds

  • Press and drag to draw a rectangle. Tangerines whose center falls inside get selected.
  • Release when the selected numbers total exactly ten. Nine or eleven does nothing.
  • Cleared tangerines leave holes, and holes are ignored by later selections. The board gets easier as it empties.
  • The clock stops at 120 seconds and your score is final.

Your score is the number of tangerines you clear, multiplied by a combo bonus that grows while you keep clearing within two seconds of the last clear. The full multiplier table is on the rules page, and the habits that keep a combo alive are in the strategy guide.
