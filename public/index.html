About Tangerine 10

Tangerine 10 is a free browser puzzle built on one rule: drag a box around tangerines whose numbers add up to exactly ten, and they pop. You get 120 seconds. No sign-up, no download, no app store.

Every game deals a fresh grid of tangerines numbered 1 to 9 — 170 of them on desktop, 168 on a phone — so you never replay the same board. Your run is saved to your own browser, and your best score goes to the global leaderboard above.

The rules in thirty seconds

  • Press and drag to draw a rectangle. Tangerines whose center falls inside get selected.
  • Release when the selected numbers total exactly ten. Nine or eleven does nothing.
  • Cleared tangerines leave holes, and holes are ignored by later selections. The board gets easier as it empties.
  • The clock stops at 120 seconds and your score is final.

How scoring actually works

This trips up most new players. Your score is not the sum of the numbers you cleared. It is how many tangerines you removed, multiplied by your combo bonus.

Combo streakMultiplierClearing 4 tangerines earns
1×14 points
2 – 4×1.56 points
5 – 9×28 points
10 – 19×312 points
20+×520 points

The combo resets two seconds after your last clear. That single detail decides most games. Taking a small match immediately beats hunting for a bigger one and losing the streak.

Three tips that work straight away

  • Memorize the five pairs. 1+9, 2+8, 3+7, 4+6, 5+5. Pairs need the smallest box and are the fastest way to keep a streak alive.
  • Sweep in horizontal bands. Work two or three rows at a time instead of scanning the whole grid. The rectangles you need are short and wide.
  • Abandon stranded nines. A 9 only ever pairs with a 1. If no 1 is reachable, leave it and spend the seconds elsewhere.
